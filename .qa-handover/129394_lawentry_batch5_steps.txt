
### 134968 — Verify that a valid External Link URL is accepted and saved
tags: ABOUT, Ai_MCP_Injected, Automation, Control_Panel, Functional-Low, Redirect
  1. Open a law entry record under the Chamber's Law page in Liferay CMS
     -> Law entry record opens with the External Link URL field available
  2. Enter 'https://www.almeezan.qa/LawView.aspx?opt&LawID=2541' as the External Link URL and click Save
     -> Value is accepted with no validation error and the success toast is shown
  3. Reload the law entry record
     -> External Link URL reads 'https://www.almeezan.qa/LawView.aspx?opt&LawID=2541' with its query string preserved

### 134969 — Verify that an invalid External Link URL is rejected with the valid-URL message

  1. Open a law entry record under the Chamber's Law page in Liferay CMS
     -> Law entry record opens with the External Link URL field available

### 134970 — Verify that the External Link URL accepts exactly 500 characters and rejects 501
tags: ABOUT, Ai_MCP_Injected, Automation, Control_Panel, Functional-Low, Redirect
  1. Open a law entry record under the Chamber's Law page in Liferay CMS
     -> Law entry record opens with the External Link URL field available
  2. Enter a valid URL exactly 500 characters long as the External Link URL and click Save
     -> The 500-character URL is accepted and saved with the success toast shown
  3. Extend the same URL to 501 characters and click Save
     -> The 501st character is not accepted — the field caps at 500 characters or a max-length validation error is shown and the value is not saved

  1. Open a law entry record under the Chamber's Law page in Liferay CMS
     -> Law entry record opens with the External Link URL field empty
