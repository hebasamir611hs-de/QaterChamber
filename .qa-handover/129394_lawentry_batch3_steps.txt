
### 134952 — Verify that a valid English Law Title is accepted and saved
tags: ABOUT, Ai_MCP_Injected, Automation, Control_Panel, Functional-Low
  1. Open a law entry record under the Chamber's Law page in Liferay CMS
     -> Law entry record opens with Law Title (EN) editable
  2. Enter 'Establishment of the Qatar Chamber of Commerce and Industry' in Law Title (EN) and click Save
     -> Value is accepted with no validation error and the success toast is shown
  3. Reload the law entry record
     -> Law Title (EN) reads 'Establishment of the Qatar Chamber of Commerce and Industry'

### 134953 — Verify that an empty English Law Title is rejected on save
tags: ABOUT, Ai_MCP_Injected, Automation, Control_Panel, Functional-Low
  1. Open a law entry record with Law Title (EN) populated
     -> Law entry record opens with Law Title (EN) populated
  2. Clear Law Title (EN) completely and click Save
     -> Save is blocked with a required-field validation error against Law Title (EN) and no success toast appears
  3. Reload the law entry record
     -> Law Title (EN) retains its previous value

### 134954 — Verify that the English Law Title accepts exactly 200 characters and rejects 201
tags: ABOUT, Ai_MCP_Injected, Automation, Control_Panel, Functional-Low
  1. Open a law entry record under the Chamber's Law page in Liferay CMS
     -> Law entry record opens with Law Title (EN) editable
  2. Enter a 200-character string in Law Title (EN) and click Save
     -> The 200-character value is accepted and saved with the success toast shown
  3. Enter a 201-character string in Law Title (EN) and click Save
     -> The 201st character is not accepted — the field caps at 200 characters or a max-length validation error is shown and the value is not saved

### 134955 — Verify that a whitespace-only English Law Title is rejected on save
tags: ABOUT, Ai_MCP_Injected, Automation, Control_Panel, Functional-Low
  1. Open a law entry record under the Chamber's Law page in Liferay CMS
     -> Law entry record opens with Law Title (EN) editable
  2. Enter four space characters in Law Title (EN) and click Save
     -> Save is blocked with a required-field validation error and no success toast appears
  3. Reload the law entry record
     -> Law Title (EN) retains its previous value and is not stored as blank or spaces
