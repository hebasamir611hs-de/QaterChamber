
### 134940 — Verify that a valid SVG Law Entry Icon under 2 MB is accepted
tags: ABOUT, Ai_MCP_Injected, Automation, Control_Panel, Functional-Low
  1. Open a law entry record under the Chamber's Law page in Liferay CMS
     -> Law entry record opens with the Law Entry Icon field available
  2. Upload law-icon.svg (SVG, 40 KB) to the Law Entry Icon field and click Save
     -> Upload is accepted with no validation error and the success toast is shown
  3. Reload the law entry record
     -> The Law Entry Icon field shows law-icon.svg as the stored image

### 134941 — Verify that a Law Entry Icon in an unsupported format is rejected
tags: ABOUT, Ai_MCP_Injected, Automation, Control_Panel, Functional-Low
  1. Open a law entry record under the Chamber's Law page in Liferay CMS
     -> Law entry record opens with the Law Entry Icon field available
  2. Upload law-icon.gif (GIF, 80 KB) to the Law Entry Icon field
     -> Upload is rejected with an image-format validation error naming JPG, PNG and SVG as the permitted formats, and the previously stored icon is unchanged

### 134942 — Verify that a Law Entry Icon above 2 MB is rejected at the size boundary
tags: ABOUT, Ai_MCP_Injected, Automation, Control_Panel, Functional-Low
  1. Open a law entry record under the Chamber's Law page in Liferay CMS
     -> Law entry record opens with the Law Entry Icon field available
  2. Upload icon-2mb.png (PNG, exactly 2 MB) to the Law Entry Icon field and click Save
     -> The exactly-2 MB file is accepted and saved with the success toast shown
  3. Upload icon-oversize.png (PNG, 2.1 MB) to the same field
     -> The 2.1 MB file is rejected with 'Image size must not exceed 2 MB.' and the stored icon is unchanged
  4. Upload icon-1_5mb.png (PNG, 1.5 MB) to the same field
     -> The 1.5 MB file is accepted and saved - the field is not capped at the 1 MB figure printed in the PBI field table

### 134943 — Verify that saving a law entry without an icon is blocked
tags: ABOUT, Ai_MCP_Injected, Automation, Control_Panel, Functional-Low
  1. Create a new law entry under the Chamber's Law page with all other mandatory fields completed and the Law Entry Icon field empty
     -> The law entry form is completed except for the icon
  2. Click Save
     -> Save is blocked with a required-field validation error against Law Entry Icon and no success toast appears
  3. Check the Law Entry list
     -> No new law entry record appears in the Law Entry list

### 134944 — Verify that a valid English Law Number is accepted and saved

### 134946 — Verify that the English Law Number accepts exactly 100 characters and rejects 101

  1. Open a law entry record under the Chamber's Law page in Liferay CMS
     -> Law entry record opens with Law Number (EN) editable
  2. Enter a 100-character string in Law Number (EN) and click Save
     -> The 100-character value is accepted and saved with the success toast shown
  3. Enter a 101-character string in Law Number (EN) and click Save
